Privacy Policy for Clutch Kicker

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Beck Creations Co. ("we," "us," or "our") built the Clutch Kicker app (the "App") as a commercial application. This Privacy Policy is meant to help you understand what data we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

1. Information We Collect

Clutch Kicker is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require you to create a proprietary account with us, and we do not directly collect personal identifying information such as your name, phone number, or email address.

However, the App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device to provide essential game functionality:

  • Google Play Games Services: To provide global leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saving, the App integrates with Google Play Games. If you choose to log in, Google Play Games will process your gameplay data, profile identifier, and progression. You can manage your Play Games profile and privacy settings directly through your Google account.
  • In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play Billing): The App offers premium unlockables. All financial transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We use RevenueCat to validate and manage these purchases. RevenueCat collects and processes a unique customer identifier and your purchase history (Financial Information) to ensure you maintain access to the items you have bought. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or payment details.
    RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by the third-party services integrated into the App is used strictly to:

  • Process, validate, and restore in-app purchases.
  • Track gameplay progression, achievements, and leaderboard rankings via Google Play Games.
  • Maintain and improve the basic functionality of the App.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide you with the App's services.

  • Locally Stored Data: You can delete all locally stored game data and preferences at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's cache in your device settings.
  • Third-Party Data: If you wish to request the deletion of your purchase history managed by RevenueCat or your Play Games progression, please contact us at the email provided below, and we will initiate the deletion process with those service providers.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your data, and we are committed to using commercially acceptable means of protecting it. All data transmitted between the App and our third-party service providers (Google and RevenueCat) is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
